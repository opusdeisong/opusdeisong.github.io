I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Yohan Jo in the HOLI Lab.

I study how LLMs behave and how to make them work better with people. My research examines their values and behavioral patterns [ACL ’25] [arXiv ’26], their ability to simulate people [EMNLP ’26], and their interactions with users and tools [ICLR ’26] [ACL ’26].
