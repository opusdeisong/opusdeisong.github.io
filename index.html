I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Yohan Jo in the HOLI Lab.

I study how language models and agents behave in practice. I build evaluations of tool use, user interaction, and the expression of values and personas, with the goal of making agents more reliable and simulations of people more faithful.

Research

Agent behavior and reliability

When do agents ask questions, trust a tool, or change their approach? I study these behavioral tendencies and failures that emerge when tools are unreliable or users are hard to collaborate with.

Related work: PA-Tool, NCUser

Values, personas, and human simulation

Do a model's stated values and persona predict its actions? I study this gap and how simulated people and fictional characters should respond to changes in context and time.

Related work: Value Portrait, Virtual Respondents

AgentHabit: Characterizing Distinct Behaviors of Agents on Everyday Tasks

Task success tells only part of the story. We profile 18 models along 23 behavioral axes across 86 everyday tasks, revealing distinct tendencies in how agents ask, act, and explain.

Preprint · 2026 Co-first author

Agents' Overreliance on Unreliable Tools

Agents often pass incorrect tool outputs to users even when their reasoning notices a conflict. We test this across 14 models and find that verification prompts reduce the problem.

EMNLP 2026 · Main Co-first author

ArcANE: Do Role-Playing Language Agents Stay in Character at the Right Time?

A character's beliefs and motivations change over a story. ArcANE evaluates whether role-playing agents reflect the character at the right point in that story.

Human Psychometric Questionnaires Mischaracterize LLM Behavior

A model's questionnaire answers can diverge from its responses to realistic user queries. Persona effects on questionnaires do not reliably carry over to generated behavior.
