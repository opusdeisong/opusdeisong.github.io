I'm an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Yohan Jo in the HOLI Lab (Human-Oriented Language Intelligence).

I study how AI agents behave and how to shape their behavior. My long-term goal is to build capable, socially intelligent agents aligned with human needs and values.

Research Interests

  1. 1

    [Agent Behavior] How agents work, decide, and interact

    I examine how agents make decisions, use tools, and interact with users, and how to make these behaviors more reliable.

    • how they approach everyday tasks arXiv'26
    • how they use and trust tools ACL-Main'26 arXiv'26
    • how we measure their values and behavior ACL-Main'25 EACL-Findings'26
  2. 2

    [Human-like Agents] How agents model people and fictional characters

    I'm also interested in agents that capture the values and personalities of people and fictional characters, including how their behavior changes across situations and over time.

    • whether personas carry over to behavior EMNLP-Main'26
    • how characters change over time EMNLP-Main'26

My research asks: How can we understand and shape agent behavior across tasks and social contexts?
